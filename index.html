Status of this material

This is a proposed operating model, prepared as a candidate working draft, July 2026. The maturity of each element is stated on its page. No element should be read as approved by the Board unless stated.

Thresholds, delegation levels and remuneration bands set out in this operating model are recommendations calibrated to a fund of approximately $58 million a year, and remain subject to Board decision. Intellectual property positions require legal review before adoption. Further detail on the evidence and assumptions underlying the model is set out under Evidence, assumptions and design choices.
